UAMSWP Video Directory is a WordPress plugin that adds a **Videos** post type, a **Video Categories** taxonomy, and the templates that show a single video and the video archive.

This wiki holds the documentation: a walkthrough, task guides, the reference, and troubleshooting. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-Video-Directory#readme) keeps the overview, the requirements, installation and the development notes.

## Getting started

- [Installation (README)](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-Video-Directory#installation): add the plugin to a WordPress site and activate it.
- [Adding your first video](Adding-your-first-video.md): from an active plugin to a video on its own page and in the archive.
